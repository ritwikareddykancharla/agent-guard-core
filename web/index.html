§01 · Problem Statement

Why Multi-Agent Systems Leak PII, Escalate Privileges & Forget Guardrails

Four production failure modes across AWS Applied AI Core Services, Luna GenAI Gateway, and Amazon Connect multi-agent architectures—and how AgentGuard & ContextCore eliminates them.

💡 In Plain English — What Problem Does AgentGuard & ContextCore Solve?

Suppose a doctor or corporate treasurer asks an AI orchestrator to resolve a ticket, and that orchestrator delegates work to specialized sub-agents across 65 conversation turns. Without hard runtime boundaries, four bad things happen: (1) Confused-Deputy Escalation: a sub-agent inherits broad admin permissions or approves a $25,000 wire transfer on its own; (2) Context-Window PII Leakage: raw patient SSNs, credit cards, and AWS keys get sent straight to third-party LLM APIs alongside prompt-injection traps hidden inside logs; (3) Context Amnesia: simple sliding-window truncation forgets a critical SLA or GDPR rule stated back in Turn 3 (0.0% FIFO recall); and (4) Runaway Spend: sending every routine lookup to a slow reasoning model burns 10× your inference budget.

AgentGuard & ContextCore wraps the entire lifecycle: cryptographic capability tokens narrow permissions at every Agent-to-Agent (A2A) hop (23/23 escalations blocked) and pause risky tools for human sign-off; a reversible pre-context firewall swaps PII for <PII:...> tokens (360/360 redacted, 40/40 injections blocked, 0.0% false positives); a 3-tier memory MCP recalls early enterprise constraints at 100.0% Recall@4 (5.17× compression); and a semantic-cached multi-model gateway cuts cost by 89.58% (86.8 ms vs 920.0 ms).

Failure 01 · A2A Escalation

Sub-Agent Scope Creep & Unapproved Mutations

Passing static API keys across multi-agent chains lets compromised sub-agents broaden tool wildcards, inflate USD budgets, or execute WIRE_TRANSFER and PROD_DEPLOY calls without human sign-off.

Failure 02 · PII & Hijacks

Raw PII Exposure & Indirect Prompt Injection

Feeding untrusted tickets or log snippets directly into LLM prompts leaks SSN, PATIENT_MRN, CREDIT_CARD, and AKIA keys while allowing ChatML/Base64 overrides to hijack tool calls.

Failure 03 · FIFO Amnesia

Sliding Windows Drop Early Enterprise Rules

Under a tight 220-token working window across 65 turns, naive FIFO truncation loses 8/8 early SLA, GDPR residency, and KMS rotation constraints (0.0% recall).

Failure 04 · Cost & Loops

Static Reasoning Spend & Stuck Tool Loops

Routing paraphrased or routine lookups to frontier reasoning models costs $0.0750 per 100 requests at 920 ms latency—and stuck agents burn quota repeating identical tool calls.

§03 · Implementation

Four Zero-Dependency Python Modules (agent_guard/)

Each module pairs plain-English intuition with exact cryptographic token math, firewall tokenization, TF-IDF memory promotion, and Pareto gateway routing.

MODULE 01 · A2A CAPABILITY & JIT PROXY agent_guard/identity_delegation.py

Monotonic HMAC-SHA256 Capability Attenuation & JIT Hold

Plain-English Intuition: Think of an A2A capability token like a hotel keycard handed to a sub-agent: the parent can restrict it to Room 402 for 10 minutes, and the sub-agent can never edit the card to unlock the penthouse or raise its spending cap.
Under the Hood (Crypto & Glob Math): Signs canonical JSON (human_principal_id, agent_id, parent_agent_id, allowed_tool_globs, allowed_resource_arns, max_cost_usd, exp) with HMAC-SHA256. Verifies strict subset attenuation (23/23 escalation attacks blocked in 1.25 ms), suspends WIRE_TRANSFER / PROD_DEPLOY / IAM_MUTATE / DELETE (4/4 held), and verifies 37/37 SHA-256 audit links.
MODULE 02 · REVERSIBLE PII & INJECTION FIREWALL agent_guard/pii_firewall.py

Session-Vault Tokenization & Adversarial Payload Quarantine

Plain-English Intuition: The LLM only needs to know *"send an SMS to <PII:PHONE_001>"*—it never needs the actual phone number! Only when the approved SMS tool is called does our proxy swap <PII:PHONE_001> back in, while keeping SSNs and credit cards locked away.
Under the Hood (6-Class Vault + Fuzzer): Redacts SSN, AWS_ACCESS_KEY, CREDIT_CARD, EMAIL, PHONE, and PATIENT_MRN (360/360 entities = 100.0% recall & 100.0% selective rehydration across 60 multi-entity cases) while quarantining ChatML delimiter spoofing, instruction overrides, and Base64 tool smuggling (40/40 blocked, 0.0% FPR on 20 benign controls in 3.59 ms).
MODULE 03 · 3-TIER HIERARCHICAL MEMORY MCP agent_guard/memory_mcp.py

Working Window + Episodic Decay + Semantic TF-IDF Promotion

Plain-English Intuition: Humans don't memorize 65 transcripts word-for-word; we keep the last few sentences in short-term memory, summarize recent chapters, and store permanent rules (like *"EU data stays in Frankfurt"*) in long-term memory with citations.
Under the Hood (3-Tier Eviction Engine): Enforces a 220-token Tier 1 Working budget (1 pinned guardrail + 6 recent turns), compacts 57 evicted turns into Tier 2 Episodic summaries with exponential recency decay, and promotes high-salience policy/SLA facts into Tier 3 Semantic Memory (8 facts)—compressing 1,819 raw tokens to 352 (5.17×) with **100.0% Recall@4 vs 0.0% FIFO**.
MODULE 04 · PARETO GATEWAY & KILL-SWITCHES agent_guard/model_gateway.py · web_api.py

Tenant-Isolated Semantic Cache, Tier Router & Loop Breaker

Plain-English Intuition: Repeated or paraphrased support questions get answered in 1.2 ms from a tenant-isolated cache, simple lookups go to nova-micro, hard audits escalate to reasoning models, and stuck agents repeating the same tool 3 times get unplugged immediately.
Under the Hood (100-Request Pareto Suite): Hits 84.0% tenant-scoped Semantic Cache (1.2 ms, $0.00), routes 10 fast-tier and 8 reasoning-tier calls, cutting total spend from $0.075018 to **$0.007814 (89.58% saved)** and mean latency from 920.0 ms to **86.75 ms**, plus canonical-args SHA-256 loop & budget circuit breakers.
